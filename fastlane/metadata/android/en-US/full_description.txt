Explore the countries and capitals of Europe in a simple offline quiz.

Choose the capital for a country or the country for a capital. Play ten-question rounds with four answers, or switch on Hard difficulty and type your answer to practise spelling. Immediate feedback helps you learn.

Includes 51 places across Europe and selected border cases: non-EU countries, microstates, Cyprus, the South Caucasus, Kazakhstan, and Kosovo. The game supports English and German, with a language switch in the app. Flags appear beside country names only.

No account, ads, tracking, or internet access required.
